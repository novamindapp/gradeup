Cumulative GPA Calculator

Finished a semester? Enter your current GPA and how many credits you have completed, then add your new courses — GradeUp works out your new cumulative GPA.

New courses

CourseGradeCredits

New courses use the standard 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, C = 2.0, D = 1.0, F = 0.0, with plus/minus steps). If your school reports weighted grades for new courses, use the weighted GPA calculator first and enter those results here as needed.

How cumulative GPA works

Your GPA is really total grade points divided by total credits. So the calculator turns your current GPA back into points, adds the new courses' points, and divides by the new total credits.

New cumulative GPA = (Current GPA × Completed credits + New grade points) ÷ (Completed credits + New credits)

Worked example

Current GPA 3.4 with 45 credits = 153.0 points. New courses: A (4.0), 3 credits → 12.0 points; B (3.0), 3 credits → 9.0 points. New total: (153.0 + 21.0) ÷ (45 + 6) = 174 ÷ 51 = 3.41 cumulative GPA.
