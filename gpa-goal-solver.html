GPA Goal Solver

What GPA do I need next semester to reach my target? Enter your current GPA, your completed credits, the GPA you want, and how many credits you plan to take — GradeUp tells you the exact semester GPA you need.

If the needed GPA is above 4.0, the goal is not reachable next semester — the calculator also shows the highest GPA straight As would give you.

How the goal solver works

It works out how many total grade points your target requires, subtracts the points you already have, and divides what is left by your planned credits.

Needed GPA = (Target GPA × (Completed credits + Planned credits) − Current GPA × Completed credits) ÷ Planned credits

Worked example

Current GPA 3.1 with 30 credits, target 3.5, planned 15 credits: (3.5 × 45 − 3.1 × 30) ÷ 15 = (157.5 − 93) ÷ 15 = 64.5 ÷ 15 = 4.3 — above a 4.0, so this target is not reachable next semester. With straight As the highest reachable GPA is (3.1 × 30 + 4.0 × 15) ÷ 45 = 153 ÷ 45 = 3.40. A realistic target would be 3.4 or lower, or the same 3.5 spread over two semesters.
