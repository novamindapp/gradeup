How the formula works

Your final grade is a weighted average: your current grade counts for everything except the final, and the final counts for its weight. Working backwards:

Required final score = (Target grade − Current grade × (1 − Final weight)) ÷ Final weight

Worked example

Your current grade is 88%, the final is worth 30%, and you want an A (90%). Required score = (90 − 88 × 0.70) ÷ 0.30 = (90 − 61.6) ÷ 0.30 = 28.4 ÷ 0.30 = 94.7%. So you need a 94.7% on the final to finish with a 90%.

Tip: if your teacher drops the lowest test or offers extra credit, your "current grade" for this calculator should be the grade after those adjustments.
