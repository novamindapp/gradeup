About GradeUp

GradeUp is a free set of tools for students who want a straight answer to one question: what do I need? What do I need on my final to get an A? What GPA do I need next semester to reach my target?

The tools are:

  • Final Grade Calculator — the exact final exam score you need, plus a what-if table for every letter grade.
  • Weighted GPA Calculator — Regular, Honors (+0.5), and AP / IB (+1.0) courses.
  • Cumulative GPA Calculator — your updated GPA after new courses.
  • GPA Goal Solver — the semester GPA you need to hit a target, and whether it is reachable.

Everything runs in your browser. There is no account, no sign-up, and nothing you type is sent to a server. Grade scales vary by school, so use the results as a planning estimate and check your school's official scale for final numbers.
