Privacy Policy

Short version: GradeUp does not collect your grades. There is no sign-up and no account. Every calculation runs in your browser on your own device, and nothing you type into a calculator is sent to a server or stored by us.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email, school, or grades, and we do not store anything you enter. Closing the page erases your inputs.

Advertising

GradeUp may display ads in the future. If an ad network is added, that provider may set its own cookies to serve ads, as described in its own privacy policy. The calculators themselves still run entirely in your browser.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
