Weighted GPA Calculator

Calculate your weighted GPA with Honors and AP classes. Add each course, pick its letter grade and credit count, and set its level — Regular, Honors (+0.5), or AP / IB (+1.0).

Courses with 0 or blank credits are ignored. Grade points use the standard 4.0 scale: A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3, B = 3.0, B− = 2.7, C+ = 2.3, C = 2.0, C− = 1.7, D+ = 1.3, D = 1.0, F = 0.0.

How weighted GPA works

Each course contributes grade points × credits. A harder class is worth more: Honors adds 0.5 and AP / IB adds 1.0 to the grade points before multiplying by credits. Then divide total points by total credits.

Weighted GPA = (Sum of grade points × credits, with level bonus) ÷ (Total credits)

Worked example

Math, AP, A (4.0 + 1.0 = 5.0), 4 credits → 20.0 points. English, Honors, B+ (3.3 + 0.5 = 3.8), 3 credits → 11.4 points. History, Regular, A− (3.7), 3 credits → 11.1 points. Total: 42.5 points ÷ 10 credits = 4.25 weighted GPA.

Note: some schools cap weighted GPA at 5.0 or use a different bump. This calculator shows the uncapped value — check your school's scale for official numbers.
